## The randomizer: what it does now
- At the start of a new game it shuffles the classes, the growth rates and the starting stats of the 49 playable people.
  - Growth totals and stat totals stay the same, and stats stay within the class caps.
- Any weapon a unit carries that its new class cannot use is swapped for a normal shop weapon of the same rank. The spoiler file lists every swap.
- Some people keep their classes: Corrin, Azura, the four royals with legendary weapons, Mozu, and the story people.
- Each route (Birthright, Conquest, Revelation) still has a staff user, a door opener and a dragon-vein user.
- Children are left out, and no new people are added.
- The same seed always gives exactly the same game.
- The robot checked it in its own test game:
  - In memory, all 49 people matched the plan, with 0 mismatches.
  - Gunter was a Strategist and Felicia a Bow Knight on the Chapter 2 map.
  - With the randomizer removed, the game is exactly the original again.

## How to start one
1. Double-click **PLAY RANDOMIZED.bat** in the repo folder.
2. Pick "New randomized game", then type a seed or let it make one, and pick the switches (classes, growths, starting stats).
3. Next time, pick "Continue randomized game" and choose the seed.

Each randomized game lives in its own folder, play/randomizer/<seed>/, with its own saves. Your normal saves, mods and cheats are never used or touched. Deleting that folder deletes that randomized game.
