## What the launcher does (ELI5)
Think of it as a checklist at the door. It has four rows: your game file, Seal of Flames (optional), the game program, and the Randomizer (optional).
- **Game file:** you can pick one of three ways:
  - 1 looks in the usual folders (roms, Downloads, Desktop, Documents; at most 3 folders deep and 30 seconds);
  - 2 opens the Windows file window;
  - 3 lets you type or drag the path.
  
  A wrong file gets one plain sentence saying why. The right file's place is remembered in `play\launcher.json`, and the file itself stays where it is.
- **Seal of Flames:** the window prints the GameBanana address and never downloads anything. S skips it. P points to the zip you downloaded.
- **Program:** Z points to your Zakuro folder. Y only checks what a build needs. The window never starts a build.
- **Ready:** Enter goes on, Q starts nothing.
- Your own `play\config.json` is never changed by it.
