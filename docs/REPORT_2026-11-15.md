  Units lost while a helper tried moves ahead were always undone. The rule "a lost unit is never kept" held.
- **Conquest chapter 9 was finished here.** Both Conquest helpers ran out of time while their check was still
  running, so no check ever finished. I replayed it twice with the original program: the game memory came out the
  same both times, and the two in-game saves are byte for byte the same. I wrote its checkpoints (all 10 units
  alive from the moment the green unit joins to the stage-complete screen), packed its save, and marked it verified.
  Revelation 10-14 were also marked verified (their helpers had proven them but forgot the flag).
- **74 new readable functions** were written this round, in three batches: items (25: an item's record, might,
  uses, forge bonus, sell price ...), movement (24: map tiles, terrain, what a step costs, who stands where) and
  supports (25: support levels and points, family links). All read only; nothing that changes the game is on.
- **The big check found one real mistake, which is now fixed.** I rebuilt the test library and replayed every
  verified real-play route live (37 routes). Every game ended in exactly the same memory as before and every
  checkpoint was met, but one new movement reader (`cursor_terrain`, "which terrain is under the cursor") made one
  extra call that the original does not make: 866 records. The cause is a shortcut in the game's code (it jumps into
  the next function instead of calling it, and the translator glues the two together), which the movement batch's
  own test did not model. I fixed the reader and the test, rebuilt, and replayed **all 38 routes live again: 106,426
  comparisons, 0 differences of any kind.** The cheat routes, counted apart: 8,395 comparisons, 0 differences. The
  safety net reports nothing written in every run.
- **How much of the readable code real play uses:** 179 of the 203 readers (58 of the 74 new ones). Three older
  readers were seen in real play for the first time (the Revelation dragon-vein script command on chapter 12, and
  the two "attack on this terrain" readers).
- **Would the robot notice a mistake? (H6)** Not measured this round. The planted-bug test was going to take about 19-20 hours in all (its shortcut cache never applies when a round adds new recordings, and there were 2.5 times more of them), so you stopped it after 7.5 hours. Last measured: 50.6%; writers stay off. Round 11 makes it use a sample of the recordings so it fits in about an hour.
- **Kaze is not lost.** The Birthright helpers noticed Kaze was missing from chapter 16 on. The game's memory shows
  he was in the army right up to the chapter-15 clear (he did not fight in that chapter) and gone at the castle
  afterwards, with another person added at the same moment. So it is a story change after chapter 15, not a battle
  loss. Which story step does it is not traced yet.
