## 2. The judge fix, in plain words
- Tougher bosses raises a boss's HP, but never past that boss's limit.
- The checker had a 'stopped at the limit' excuse that it never really checked, so it could never fail.
- Now it works out the real limit from that game's own tables. It passes a short HP rise only if the HP sits exactly on that limit.
- Round 65's short-HP-rise passes were checked again from their saved results: Hinata, Takumi (B010), Saizo, Iago (8 labels, 7 of which had used the excuse). **All sit exactly at their real limit, so all stay PASS.** No old verdict changed.
