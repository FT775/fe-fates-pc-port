## 2. What to look at while you play

These are the things fans judge first (from Grok, docs/collab/PLAY_EVENING_GROK.md):

1. You can win a fight. Someone in your group can hit the enemy in front of them.
2. Corrin has a weapon Corrin can use.
3. The face and the name match. Tonight people keep the face of the spot they join in. A known face in a strange
   class is the shuffle working.
4. The boss can be hurt. A boss in a new class is fine.
5. Music plays. A "wrong" song is the shuffle. No sound at all is a bug.
6. You can finish the map the way it asks. If you cannot move, cannot reach the goal, or the enemy turn never
   ends, stop and tell me.
7. Nobody the chapter needs is missing.
8. If you meet a shop: odd things for sale are fine. An empty shop is a bug.

**Strange but fine** (the randomizer doing its job):

- A maid with an axe, or a knight with a bow, if they can use what they hold.
- A weapon in the bag they cannot use, as long as they have one they can.
- A boss in a class you did not expect, if your hits land.
- A strong weapon in a shop early.
- A song from another map.
- A skill that does not fit the class.
- Hair that looks a little off on someone who moved. A faint colour is known.

**Broken** (tell me):

- Corrin, or someone the map needs, has no weapon they can use.
- Your attacks can never hit, or the boss never loses health.
- The enemy turn never ends, or you cannot do anything and waiting does not help.
- The name in the box is not the face on screen.
- A shop is empty.
- The game closes, or a save from a minute ago will not load.
- No music at all.
