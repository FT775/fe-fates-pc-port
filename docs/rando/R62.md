# Round 62, randomizer track: growth floor, one-step weapons, boss classes, tougher bosses, Seal Children

**For the owner.** Every growth the randomizer makes now lands between 20% and 100% once the class is added.
"Stronger enemy weapons" goes one step up the same weapon line, chosen by a new **Difficulty** row (Easy, Normal,
Hard). Story bosses can change class now, but only within the same rank of class and only where the robot has seen
their chapter run with the new class. Tougher bosses get 10% more HP and a stronger forge per Difficulty step. This
includes Conquest's bosses who join you on the other routes, but only in their Conquest chapter. The Children row is
greyed for Seal of Flames. Nothing counts as done until you say ok (F-037).

Host code and tests: `tools/randomizer/{growth,weapons,stats,enemies,tracks,rando,ingame}.py`, `tools/play/rando.py`
(the page row), `config/randomizer/rules.json`, `tests/test_rando_r62.py` (15 tests) plus updated
`tests/test_rando_weapons.py` and `tests/test_rando_stats.py`. The robot runs went on the round-61 final build (the exe in play/game,
frozen copy in `build/r62/rando/bin`). Every run was headless and closed itself (`build/r62/rando/check/`).

## 1. Growth floor (F-049 + addendum "min 20 always")

- The floor is on the **total**: personal growth + the class's growth. Every stat of every army member and child
  ends between 20 and 100, and that includes stats that were 0. Move is not a growth and is never touched.
- It runs whenever growths are randomized: slice 1's Growths switch, a growth mode, Class growth rates, or Children.
- It is the very last step. It reads the tables a run lays (Person.csv and Job.csv over the original rows) and moves
  only the personal growth, in steps of 5, and only as far as needed.
- For a base class, the floor also keeps the totals of its two Master Seal classes in 20..100 when one personal
  value can satisfy all three. If it cannot, the class the person joins as wins. Heart Seal classes are not covered.
- In the spoiler, a "Growth floor" section lists every cell it moved. It ends with the final table: personal + class
  = total, per person and stat, read from the laid files. Its guard, "growth: floor 20-100", is in plan.json.
- While playing, the game adds a child's parent and the avatar's boon and bane on top of the table. The floor cannot
  see those.

## 2. Stronger enemy weapons: one step, by Difficulty

The ladders come from the Item table, with no names hard-coded. A ladder is the cheapest plain shop weapon at each
of ranks E, D, C and B, for each weapon sub-kind (the sub-kind carries the side), range and magic. A rung must have
higher might than the one before, and its Rating may not be lower. The game has 12 ladders. Sword, lance, axe, dagger,
bow and tome each have a Nohr ladder (Bronze, Iron, Steel, Silver) and a Hoshido ladder (Brass, Iron, Steel, Silver).
The tome ladders are Fire, Thunder, Fimbulvetr, Ragnarok and Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit Spirit.

| Difficulty | Ladder weapon | Killer, Brave, effective and other forgeable weapons |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | +1 forge | +1 forge |
| Normal | next rung if the class can hold it (a boss: its own weapon exp), else +1 forge | +1 forge |
| Hard | next rung and +1 forge, else +2 forges | +2 forges |

- Yato, legendary, personal, precious and can't-remove weapons never change. Neither do staves, stones or drops.
- Forges are capped at +7. A slot not given on a game difficulty (a negative forge byte) is left alone.
- "Stronger enemy weapons (chance %)" still decides which weapons are rolled.
- `upgrade_rank` is still accepted but does nothing (grey on the page: "replaced by Difficulty").
- Guard `enemy_upgrades_legal`, recomputed from the edits alone: the next rung of the same ladder or nothing; never on
  Easy; holdable; within a boss's own rank; the Rating not lower and the might higher (no sidegrade); forges exactly
  as the table above.
- **Robot**: run `r62fladderhard-ee61c511` (Hard, 100%) on A008, A014, B010, B015, C017 and X012. All 6 jobs passed
  (start, preparations, map, enemy look, enemy phase, battle save and reload, readback).
  - Memory shows 158 enemy weapons. Every upgraded weapon has weapon exp at least its rank (Steel to Silver: exp 96),
    so the game raises a generic enemy's exp for the next tier. The one weapon below its rank is an original drop
    (Silver Naginata in A014), which the robot never changed.
  - The forge level shows in the unit item's second word (0x4101 = equipped, +1; 0x4201 = +2).

## 4. Tougher bosses, scaled by Difficulty

- Difficulty steps: Easy 1, Normal 2, Hard 3.
- With "Tougher bosses" on, every boss gets **HP +10% x step**. The 10% is of personal + class base, rounded up and
  never past the cap.
- The weapon part is a forge on the boss's weapons. It uses the fewest forge levels whose added might reaches the
  step, read from the game's forge table (ItemRefine rows 0 and 1 say +2 might at level 1, +4 at level 2). So Easy
  and Normal get +1 level and Hard gets +2. Yato, legendary and personal weapons are never forged.
- **Round 62 check: the weapon part FAILS on Normal and Hard.** On screen a forge level adds only +1 Atk (Daniela's
  Ragnarok: +1 on Easy and Normal, +2 on Hard; Seal's Steel Lance and Javelin the same). F-049 wants +1 might per
  step (Easy 1, Normal 2, Hard 3), so Normal is 1 short and Hard is 1 short. The table read above does not match the
  screen (ItemRefine row 2 has +1 per level; which row a weapon uses, or what the columns mean, is not proven). Fix in
  the next round, proven on screen. The HP part works.
- The old story-position stat buff (linear/ease) stays on top for bosses who never join.
- Conquest's chapter-only bosses who join on other routes get only this HP and forge boost, on their Conquest chapter
  row (rules.json `army_boss_boost_routes`: "B"). The person who joins on another route is not changed: no Person
  row is written.
- **Robot**: run `r62fboosthard-a82facea` (Hard) on A014, B010, B012 and C017. All passed with 0 wrong patched cells.
  The boss HP in memory rose by exactly the plan's amount:
  - Daniela 33 -> 45;
  - Takumi 33 -> 38 (capped at his class cap);
  - Ryoma 34 -> 47;
  - Iago 38 -> 45.

  Daniela's weapon shows forge +2.

## 6. What changes for old runs

- Every page run id changes: the page answer now carries the new Difficulty row (the core's run id hashes the answer).
- The tables of a seed change when:
  - **Growths** are randomized (the default): the floor;
  - **Stronger enemy weapons** is above 0: the ladder and forges replace the old pool;
  - **Tougher bosses** is on: HP, forges, the Conquest army bosses, and the stats track's random key now carries the
    difficulty;
  - **Boss classes** is on with **enemy trees: any**: bosses now stay on their own side;
  - **Boss classes** is on: the 25 proven story bosses.
- A seed with all of these off and growths not randomized makes the same files as before (test_growths_off_no_floor).
- With the randomizer off, nothing is laid: the original game.
