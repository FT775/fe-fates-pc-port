  - Detailed forecast (the game's default): the right-hand fighter only. The left fighter's number has no free room beside it.

## What the desk part found (the game's own skip)
These numbers come from Chapter 8, turn 1, measured in frames from End turn to your next turn:
- **Off:** 728 frames.
- **Move:** 543 frames (75%). The camera stops following every walk.
- **All:** 418 frames (57%). A dark "Skipping..." screen.
- **Start key:** ends the turn of the army that is moving. During a fight close-up the game ignores it. The green ally army's turn still plays unless you press Start again.
- **What stays the same:** units, HP and fight dice. **What changes:** the game's general dice move once per frame, so later enemy choices can differ. Taking more or less time on your own turn does the same. That is why the help line says so.
