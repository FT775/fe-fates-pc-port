# Round 58: randomizer for every path, chapter select, and the everything-on check

Status: built and checked by the robot. Nothing is committed, and nothing counts as done until you say ok.

## What is new
- **Seal of Flames randomizer.** The Randomizer page now shows when you pick Seal of Flames, and it randomizes from the mod's own tables.
- **Chapter select for the robot** (cheat track only). It starts any chapter from a clear save, so every chapter can be tested without a slow playthrough.
- **Character shuffle, music and Nuzlocke** are in the command-line tool and in PLAY RANDOMIZED. In PLAY RANDOMIZED, Nuzlocke still says "not yet". On the in-game page all three are still grey.
- **Route-variant joiners** (the same person joining again on another route) now get their new class too (your OK, F-046).

## Per path: what broke (and was fixed), what passed
The check found three real bugs. All three are fixed on every path:
1. Seal seeds with Join stats off were refused, because the mod's own Faceless has luck -1. Fixed in the guard.
2. A randomized battle save loaded from the title was taken for a New Game: the original tables came back and HP was cut. Fixed in engine patch 0014.
3. Some joiners (Rinkah and Kaze at Birthright chapter 4) joined with no weapon. Fixed in engine patch 0015.

There was no crash or fault in 553 robot runs. **Reviewer correction:** almost all of these runs used the build from before fix 3 (patch 0015). Only 4 runs are on the final build. One memory mismatch is still open: Birthright chapter 4 with the second seed (all2) had its joiners' cells wrong, and it was never re-run after the fix (the first seed was re-run and is right). The counts below are therefore from the earlier build. Round 59 re-runs everything on the final build.

- **Birthright:** 34 chapters fully pass (preparations, map, enemy phase, save and reload). Chapters 2-6 only reach the start of the map through recorded routes (no enemy phase or save there), because a direct start gets stuck. The robot could not reach A007, A016, A017, X009, X010, X012 and X014; these are robot problems, not game problems.
- **Conquest:** 28 of 30 pass. Not reached: B007, B016.
- **Revelation:** 19 of 22 pass. Not reached: C007, C016, C017.
- **Seal of Flames:** 35 of 41 chapters reach the preparations, the map and the enemy phase. **Save and reload fail in Seal.** With the randomizer off they fail the same way on the 10 chapters tried (R002, R005, R010, R016, R019, R024, S002, S006, Y001, Y005). Ten more chapters (R013, R018, R020-R023, R025, S005, S010, Y004) failed only with it on and had no off run, so the randomizer is not ruled out there. It needs its own look. Not reached: R011, R012, S007, S009, Y002, Y003.
- **Normal game:** with the randomizer off, the new exe gives the same memory as the old one (45 of 45 checks).

## How to undo
Close the game. Then copy these two files from build\backup\ back to play\game\, dropping the date suffix:
- Fire Emblem Fates.exe.2026-11-20-pre-sofrando
- build-record.json.2026-11-20-pre-sofrando

That gives you the game from before this round. The in-between builds are kept too, under the suffixes -pre-r58fix, -r58-0014a and -r58-0014.
