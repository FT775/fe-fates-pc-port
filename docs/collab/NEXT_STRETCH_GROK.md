# After round 78

**For the owner.** The next ambitious step is not another switch on the page. It is a way for someone who already has the official program to make a randomized game, without us shipping a library that has no licence. Rewind and a mouse come after that. A census of functions does not.

Status 2026-11-24. Sizes below that are not from a counted round are estimates. Do not ship the recompiler until its author publishes a licence or says yes. Zakuro itself is MIT.

## The seven that matter

1. **A seed kit for the official program.** About 7 hours, no engine. A small zip. No game in it. It asks before it installs, and Undo puts the old files back. This is the download we can ship while the licence is open. Ideas 14 and 23 are this one thing.
2. **Each mod is its own game.** About 9 hours. Seal of Flames is the recipe. Another fan mod gets its own saves and the port's defaults. The randomizer stays off until that mod has a rules file. Do not stack two mods in the first version. Stacking is the later, harder half.
3. **A seed that can be won.** About 7 hours. No engine. Before a seed is offered, it still has someone who can open a door, fly, and heal. The page says easy, fair, or harsh. Refuse or re-roll only if the player says yes.
4. **Rewind.** About 9 hours. Engine. Your OK. Hold a key, go back to the start of the turn, up to 8 turns. Grey in a Nuzlocke. The crash net that offers "continue from that turn" rides this same build. Do not build it twice.
5. **Enemy phase you can speed up.** About 12 hours if alone. Engine. Your OK. Normal, twice, or four times, and Start skips. This is what makes a full run bearable. A helpers screen of gold and double experience waits. Those saves must say "Assist used", and they stay grey in a Nuzlocke.
6. **One screen for a TV or a Deck.** About 14 hours. Engine. Your OK. The bottom screen pops in. The right stick is the touch pointer. Not before rewind. Fates leans on that screen, so this is the difference between a desk and a couch.
7. **Mouse on the map.** About 13 hours. Engine. Your OK. After you have played. Click a tile, click a target, right-click goes back. One engine round, not two.
