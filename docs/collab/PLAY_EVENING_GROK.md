# Tonight's seed

**For the owner.** Play until the choice of a side, and one map after you pick Birthright if chapter 6 is that choice. Stop there. You are judging whether the seed feels like a game, not whether every switch fired.

## What fans notice first

1. You can finish a fight. Someone in the starting group can hit the enemy in front of them.
2. Corrin has a weapon they can swing. A strange class is fine. No usable weapon is not.
3. The face and the name match. This seed keeps the face. A familiar face in a weird class is the shuffle working.
4. The boss can be hurt. A different class is fine. A boss that cannot be damaged is not.
5. A shop has something in it, if you open one. Odd stock is fine. An empty shop is not.
6. Music plays. The "wrong" tune is the shuffle. Silence is a bug.
7. You can still win the map the way it asks. If you cannot move, cannot reach the goal, or the turn never comes back, stop and write that down.
8. Nobody the chapter needs is missing. Corrin, and the people the early maps put next to you, should still be there.

## Fine, just odd

- A maid with an axe, or a knight with a bow, if they can still use what they hold.
- A weapon in the bag they cannot equip, as long as they have one they can.
- A boss in a class you did not expect, if your hits land.
- A shop that sells a weapon you would not have seen this early.
- Battle music from another map.
- A skill that does not match the class fantasy.
- Hair that looks a little off. Faint is known. Invisible under a hit flash is the known limit, not a new bug.

## Broken, write it down

- Corrin, or anyone the map needs, has no weapon they can use.
- You attack and it can never hit, or the boss never loses health.
- The enemy turn does not come back.
- The name in the box is not the face on the screen.
- A shop you were sent to is empty.
- You cannot take an action, and waiting does not help.
- The game closes, or a save from a minute ago will not load.
- No music at all.

One line in chat is enough: what chapter, what you pressed, and whether it was odd or stuck.
