# The two cheat endings

**For the owner.** Birthright ended because the boss was already on the map, and one poke left him on 1 health so the next hit killed him. Conquest can be the same shape. Revelation cannot. Its last boss is not on the map until two earlier parts are dead. A single poke there does not reach the ending.

Status 2026-11-17. Read: `docs/DECISIONS.md` F-030 and F-031, `docs/CHEATS.md` (the Birthright ending row), `docs/ROBOT.md` section 3y. Public guides for the two endings. No game file opened. Chapters before each ending stay real play. Only the ending may be a labeled cheat. A cheat save never starts a real-play route. The real-play ending keys stay unwritten.

Sources: [Conquest ending](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Night_Breaks_Through), [Conquest ending, Thonky](https://www.thonky.com/fire-emblem-fates/endgame-night-breaks-through), [Revelation ending](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Anankos_(chapter)), [Revelation ending, Thonky](https://www.thonky.com/fire-emblem-fates/endgame-anankos).

## What Birthright already proved

One declared poke, current health to 1, on the boss who was already standing there. One attack the forecast said would kill. Nobody lost. The map did not show a "stage complete" banner. It went straight into the ending scenes, the chapter records, the credits, a screen that waits for a key, a card that asks you to pick people, a yes/no that starts on No, then the title. No save was written. A still screen that waits for a key is not a hang. Press the key.

## Conquest

**Win:** defeat the boss who is already in the south. Not a seize. Not an escape. The copy paired with him does not have to die. Guides say the map ends when the one in front dies.

**What can block a one-poke finish:** he is on the map, so the Birthright shape can work, if the first forecast can target him. He sometimes charges a blow that hurts the whole army. That charge is a hazard, not a step the guides say you must wait for. Veins raise barriers that block most of that blow. New enemies keep coming. He does not heal unless he is allowed to kill someone on his turn. If the forecast cannot target him, or his health jumps back before the hit, stop. Do not add a second poke in that try.

**Smallest cheat:** one poke, the front boss only, current health to 1, on turn 1 of the ending, once a forecast can see him. Then one hit that the forecast says kills. Nobody lost. Do not poke the copy. Do not poke anyone in chapter 27. There is no save after chapter 27, so the ending starts from the robot's own chain, the same way Birthright did.

**After:** watch for the same waits Birthright had. A key on the end screen. A card that asks for people. A yes/no that may start on No. Then the title. Do not pack a clear save unless a prompt actually writes one. Birthright wrote none. If a prompt appears that Birthright did not have, stop and write it down. Do not press through it on a guess.

## Revelation

**Win:** the core, not the hands, and not the head. The hands are there at the start. The head appears when both hands are dead, and it does not act on that turn. The core appears when the head is dead, and it does not act on that turn. New enemies keep coming, from a different corner after each part dies. No save, and no castle, once chapter 27 has been won for real.

**What blocks a one-poke finish:** the next part is not on the map yet. Poking a hand, even to 1, only starts the sequence. Poking "the boss" at the start cannot touch the core. A script does not have to say no. The unit is simply not there.

**Smallest cheat:** three declared pokes, not one. Each is the same kind as Birthright: that part's current health to 1, then one killing hit, then wait until the next part appears before the next poke. Hands first, both of them. Then the head, on the turn it appears. Then the core, on the turn it appears. If a part's health jumps back, stop that try. Do not poke a unit that has not appeared. Chapter 27 stays real play.

**After:** same watch list as Conquest. The end screen waits for a key. Do not treat a long still screen as a hang until it has been given that key. No clear save unless a file is actually written. The title is the landing Birthright reached. If this ending stops somewhere else, that is the finding. Do not invent a save to get past it.

## Order

1. Finish the real chapters first. The cheat is only the ending.
2. Conquest can be one poke, if he can be targeted on turn 1.
3. Revelation is three pokes, one per part, each after the last part died.
4. Both stay labeled cheats. Neither writes the real-play ending key.
