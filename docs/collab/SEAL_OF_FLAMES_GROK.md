## What we may do

The license says you may download and install it. It says ask before redistributing it, modifying it and sharing the result, or using parts in another mod. Commercial use is no.

1. **Load, if you download it yourself.** Allowed. We do not host the files. A credit line names Laz14, the page, and the license. Our mod list can say "not included; download it yourself."
2. **Ship it with the port, or put it in our repo.** That is redistributing. Ask first. The files also replace the game's own data files. Those do not belong in our tree even if the author said yes to a link.
3. **Fix the known bugs, or merge it with our looks.** That is a changed copy, or parts used in another mod. Ask first. A private experiment on your own machine is yours. A fix we share is not.

We do not write to the author. If you want to ask, this is a message you can send:

May I tell players they can download Seal of Flames from your GameBanana page and load it there, with your name and the CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 line, without including your files? ## What it would take

The author's install is a folder named `0004000000179800`, the same title our port uses, Special Edition USA. Other editions are not what the page asks for. Citra's mods folder and Zakuro's are the same idea: files under `romfs` replace the game's file at the same path. Our note already says Zakuro reads `%APPDATA%\zakuro\mods\0004000000179800\romfs`. No new loader is required for a drop-in folder. A check that this path still works is hours, not a week [low].

Do not run our mods tool on top of it. The author says other mods are not meant to be used with it. Our tool also refuses to mix a whole replacement file with a cell edit. Turn our looks off first.

The robot may try it only in its own test folder, never your saves. New game, or the branch, then "Refuse to choose a side," then Chapter 6 starts. That is the boot check. One sitting, a few hours, then stop [low]. Do not play all 38 chapters to call the load done.

The mid-battle save bug is reported on Citra. The page says use save states, and save normally between chapters. We do not know if our port shows the same bug until that one try. Do not promise a fix. If it fails here too, write that down and leave their files alone.

The accessory shop and a private-quarters invite can crash. Some portraits and map markers are wrong. Challenge battles are empty. Keep our mods and any downloadable content off while it runs.
