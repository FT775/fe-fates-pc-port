# Revelation ending, turn by turn

**For the owner.** Three pokes, three hits, then stop. The last part is not on the map until the hands and the head are dead. Chapter 27 may use the movement boost. This map does not. If that boost is still on the army, do not use it to stand on a corner.

Status 2026-11-18. Read: `docs/collab/FINALES_GROK.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md` F-035. Public guides only. No game file opened. Nobody may be lost. If a part's health jumps back, stop that try. Do not add a fourth poke.

Sources: [wiki](https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Anankos_(chapter)), [Thonky](https://www.thonky.com/fire-emblem-fates/endgame-anankos), [Gamer Guides](https://www.gamerguides.com/fire-emblem-fates-revelation/guide/main-campaign/chapters-21-28/endgame-anankos), [IGN](https://www.ign.com/wikis/fire-emblem-fates/Endgame:_Anankos).

**Turn 1.** The two hands are already there, one west and one east. A ring of strong enemies starts around the army, from the upper left and on around the map. Stage just outside that ring, not on a corner. Split: one paired pair west, one east. Poke the west hand to 1 only if the forecast can see it, then one hit that kills. Same for the east hand, same turn if both can be reached. The striker is whoever the forecast says kills, paired, with a guard ready. Guides lean on Corrin's special sword. Use it if that forecast is the kill. Do not poke the head or the core. They are not there.

**The turn both hands die.** The head appears in the center and does not act that turn. New enemies switch to the northeast corner: swords, snipers, falcon knights, and staff mages. Stage south of the center, not on that corner, not under the head until the hit. Poke the head only after it has appeared. One killing hit. Then step back south.

**The turn the head dies.** The core appears in that same center spot and does not act that turn. New enemies switch again: the northwest corner sends heroes, great knights, dark knights, and flying knights; the northeast corner sends mechanics, merchants, monks, and spear mages. Same staging: south of the center, off both corners. Poke the core only after it has appeared. One killing hit. Stop. Do not clear the corners.

**After.** The map does not show a separate "stage complete" on the Birthright ending, and this one may not either. A still screen that says the end is a wait for a key, not a hang. Press it. A card asks you to pick people. A yes/no may start on No. Then watch for the title. Do not pack a clear save unless a file is actually written.

**Do not.** Poke a part that has not appeared. Poke all three at the start. Stand on a corner that just started spawning. Chase the ring. Spend a person to buy a turn. Treat a long still screen as a crash before it has been given the key.
