# Conquest, chapters 20 through the ending

**For the owner.** The slow chapters are the ones that move people against their will, or that are not won until every person is off the map. The robot should win the stated way and stop. It should not clear the rest of the map, and it should not open chests. Nobody may be lost. If a try is still going near 100 minutes, stop and keep the place.

Status 2026-11-17. Read: the latest Conquest notes in `docs/ROBOT.md` and `docs/ROUTES_SPLIT.md`. Public guides only for the maps. No game file opened. The danger tool does not count a staff as an attack. Silence, Freeze, a staff that halves health, and a staff that pulls someone across the map are still threats. A lost person is a failed try: undo that turn. Do not copy a guide that says to spend people.

## Which ones will cost time

1. **Chapter 20.** Wind moves anyone standing in it, about five tiles, at the end of the enemy turn. The look-ahead will undo a lot.
2. **Chapter 21.** Everyone has to escape, new enemies never stop, and the last people must leave on the same turn.
3. **Chapter 22.** Walls, long-range launchers, staffs, and new enemies through about turn 11.
4. **Chapter 25.** The boss does not come out. Two side rooms have to be cleared first, and grouped knives will chip a lone unit to death.
5. **Chapter 24.** A vein the boss keeps using speeds up fliers and slows everyone else. A staff that halves health is on the map. New fliers keep coming past turn 10.

Chapters 23, 26, and 27 are ordinary if the robot does not rush a door. The ending is one fight, and it is the only map that may use the one allowed cheat. Chapters 20 through 27 stay real play.

## The maps

**20. Seize.** Beat the boss and stand on his tile. Wind blows along the walkways at the end of the enemy turn, about five tiles, or to the nearest tile a person can stand on. A vein pushes the enemy instead, and the wind is quiet for that turn. On a harder setting the same vein also pushes your own people: the first vein will show which rule this save uses. Bows and flying enemies are the danger to your own fliers. Staff users can silence or freeze. New enemies come from the stairs, including after you step onto the middle and onto the boss's platform. One guide calls this a long mission where a gust can ruin a turn. Walk in pairs. Do not end a turn on a wind tile unless the next square is one you chose. Do not hunt the still mage or the chests. Kill only what blocks the tile.

**21. Everyone escapes.** The red tiles are at the north end. New monsters come from the north and the south and do not stop. They chip everyone near them after a fight they start, so kill one outright or do not let it strike. The standing stone enemies throw about five tiles and do not move; they are the ones to kill on the way, not the whole crowd. Veins freeze every enemy for a turn. Pair up so your own people are not blocking the path. Debris forces a zigzag. The boss can be walked past. The last people must step off on the same turn. If one healer is left alone, the crowd kills them and the map is lost. Do not send a fast pair ahead and leave the rest.

**22. Seize.** The boss stands on the tile, behind walls. The map is two lanes. A vein opens a wall on the other lane, so each lane needs someone who can use a vein. Launchers, used by the mechanic enemies, hit a cluster from far away. Keep healers and singers out of that range, and do not stand in a bunch. The princess uses staffs and does not need to die, but killing her stops the staffs. New enemies arrive in waves from about turn 4 through turn 11, including fliers on the east lane. Move up only as far as the next wall. Do not open the last wall until both lanes are ready.

**23. Seize.** The boss is up a stair, and he can shoot from one tile away, so standing next to him is not safe. Launchers cover the long way around. Pair a strong person with a flier and drop them on the far side so the launchers die early. The first rooms add people over the first few turns, and they rally each other. Clear the low side, heal, then take the stairs with the fast people first. Gang the boss on your turn so he never gets a shot at someone already hurt. Do not clear the wall for its own sake.

**24. Seize.** The boss sits on a vein she can use again. Her use speeds every flier and slows everyone on foot. Yours does the opposite, and yours is spent. Bring bows. A staff on this map halves someone's full health for good: stay out of its range, kill that user, then go up. New fliers, many with bows, start at turn 4 and are still coming after turn 10. Undo her vein as soon as a royal can reach one. Bows do not get the usual bonus on her. Do not chase a flier off the path.

**25. Defeat the boss.** He does not walk out, and one guide says he waits about 25 turns. Do not wait. The doors open when his two retainers are dead. Two lanes. The left lane is built so several knives hit one person, and each hit chips health even when the blow is small. Do not lure with one unit. Step up as a group, with a healer, and hit them on your turn. The other lane has bows that reach three tiles. High defense in front, then the rooms one at a time. After both retainers are down, crowd the boss from outside his range. His sword reaches past one tile. Pair the attacker. This is a boss fight, not a 25-turn camp.

**26. Seize.** Split after the first room. West is axes and monsters, some of them chip or splash. East is magic: take the sorcerers before the maids, who heal and weaken. Stone throwers are far but inaccurate; leave them until the path is clear. Do not open the south doors until both groups are at the door. Opening one early makes that room rush you, including hammers and beast-killers. Soften the big axe fighter from out of his range, then the boss. High resistance for the boss. Stand on his tile. Chests are not the win.

**27. Defeat the boss.** Stairs in the side rooms warp to the hall and leave whatever move is left, so a fast person can arrive and still act. Kill the paired champions in the side rooms first; each kill opens a stair. Three lancers punish you for hitting them. Let them strike a tough person first, then hit them on your turn. The boss halves damage and his weapon reaches three tiles. Heal, gather just outside that range, and send tough people in one after another. Do not leave a healer in that range. On a harder setting, maids in the south rooms pull someone across the whole map on turn 1. This save has been the easier setting; if a pull happens, kill that maid before anyone else in her room. There is a save at the preparations of this chapter. There is no save after it. The ending starts from here.

**Ending. Defeat the boss.** No save, no castle, and no going back. He stands in the south, paired with a copy. You do not have to kill the copy. Every so often he charges a blow that hurts the whole army. Veins raise barriers that block most of that blow. Use one before the charge, not after. New monsters come out of the bad ground and do not stop. Clear a path, then hit him while paired, with enough speed not to be hit twice, and step back. He does not heal unless he is allowed to kill someone on his turn. One cheat is allowed on this map only, and only if the real fight is stuck. Do not use it on the way up.

Nothing here is a tested route. It is the guides, cut to the rule that nobody is lost.
