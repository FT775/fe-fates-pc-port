**For the owner.** Press one button and the stat page shows growths, then press it again and the stats come back. A stronger enemy weapon is one step up, not a leap. A boss stays in a class of the same rank. The last bosses of the story do not change class.

## Growths on a button

On the unit's stat page, Select (Tab, or Back on a pad) replaces the stat numbers with growth percents. Select again puts the stats back. The bottom screen gets a Growths button that does the same thing, because a 3DS page is touched as often as it is pressed. Move does not grow, so it stays a number. The percents are the total: personal plus class. A second press can show the two parts. The cap stays off this button until you ask. Colours can wait. The first version is numbers only.

## The floor

The floor is on the total of each stat, not on personal and class separately. If the floor is 20, a stat whose personal and class add up to 14 is raised until they add to 20. HP may sit 10 points higher. Luck and Res use the same floor as the rest, so they are not the dump stats and not the only high ones. A stat that was 0 in both halves stays 0. The floor must not invent a growth the person and the class never had.

## One step stronger

The ladder, same kind and same range only:

- Nohr: Bronze, Iron, Steel, Silver.
- Hoshido: Brass, Iron, Steel, Silver.
- The same four steps for swords, lances, axes, bows, katanas, naginatas, clubs, yumi, and shuriken.
- Scrolls and tomes: the bronze, iron, steel, and silver of that line only.
- Killer, Brave, and "effective against" weapons are not on this ladder. They may take a +1 forge, and they keep the bonus.
- Yato, the legendaries, and a personal weapon do not move.

Easy: a +1 forge, if that class may hold it. Normal: the next tier, if the class's rank allows it, otherwise a +1 forge. Hard: the next tier and a +1 forge, still inside that rank. Never Bronze to Silver in one step. A forge is +1 through +7 on a weapon the class already holds. Prefer the next tier when the rank allows it. A forge is the fallback, not the show.

## Boss classes

A base boss stays a base class. A promoted boss stays promoted. Same side, same gender, a weapon that class can hold.

Never change: Garon, Anankos and his hands, head, and core, a possessed royal, a dragon form, and the singer if the scene needs the song. Public randomizers that change bosses also rewrite the scene. We do not. A boss the script names can change class only inside the same tier, and only if the scene does not check the class. If we do not know, leave it.

## Tougher bosses

One number, the difficulty step. Easy 1, Normal 2, Hard 3.

Health added is 10% times that step. Weapon might added is 1 times that step, still inside the class's rank. Conquest bosses who join on another route use this same line, on the chapter row only. The person you recruit later is not changed.
