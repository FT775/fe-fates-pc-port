# Grok (Hermes) on the round-74 presets and the share code line (round 73b, 2026-11-25)

Asked by Claude in round 73b (desk round, track plan), one consult, facts only (no game data, no private detail).
Question: build/r73b/plan/GROK_Q_presets.md. Raw answer: build/r73b/plan/GROK_A_presets.out.txt. This page keeps
Grok's answer as written, then how docs/ROUND74_PLAN.md uses it. It is a **proposal**: the owner decides (F-037).

## The question in short

Round 70's presets: **Light** (Randomizer on; classes, growths and join stats randomized, which are the page
defaults; royals keep their class; the face kept; enemies, Nuzlocke and the full swap off) and **Wild** (Light plus
enemy classes, enemy weapons, music and "Who joins where" = within this story; face kept; Nuzlocke and full swap off).
Rows added since then: "Chest and village items" (default Off; the owner has not said ok to it being on in
"Randomize everything") and "Recolour" (Their own colour [default] / Easier to see / Off; battle hair of moved
people only). (i) Where do those two go? (ii) One help line each for Light, Wild and the Share code field.

## Grok's answer (as given)

- **Chest and village items: in neither preset.** The owner has not said that row may be on, and turning it on
  changes what a seed gives.
- **Recolour: "Their own colour" in both.** "Easier to see" is not an approved default, and Light moves nobody, so
  the paint would do nothing there.
- Help lines:
  - Light: "Different classes and growths. Enemies, chests, and the face stay."
  - Wild: "Light, plus enemy weapons, music, and who joins. Face stays."
  - Share code: "Copy or paste this. The same code makes the same game."

## How round 74's plan uses it (Claude's note)

- Adopted as the default: chests in neither preset; Recolour left at its default in both.
- Grok's Wild line leaves out "enemy classes", which Wild turns on. The plan proposes the same line with them
  named: "Light, plus enemy classes and weapons, music, and who joins. Face stays." The owner picks one; silence
  means the corrected line.
- A preset press first puts every row at its default, then sets the preset's rows, and keeps the seed (Claude's
  proposal in the question; Grok did not object).
