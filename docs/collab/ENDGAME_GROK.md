# How players beat Birthright's last chapter

**For the owner.** The robot is losing time because it is clearing the map. Players do not. They walk to the boss and hit him, and they do it before the ground turns bad. This army cannot do the fancy tricks the guides like, because those items are not in the bags. Go back to turn 5 and walk north. Do not keep the turn-9 try.

## Ranked

1. **Beat the boss. Do not clear the map.** Every guide says the win is the boss, new enemies keep appearing from the marked tiles, and a sweep is wasted because nothing after this chapter is kept. On Normal the repeat wave is not endless: the wiki says the turn-7 pattern runs through turn 11. That is not a reason to wait. Waiting is how both tries died on the clock.

2. **Use what this army holds. Do not go looking.** Guides lean on the Blazing Yato, a song saved for Corrin, and dual strikes from someone standing next to the attacker. They also mention a Hexing Rod, Entrap, Freeze, Enfeeble, a hammer, tonics, and a dragon-effective sword. None of those is in the bags written down in sections 3v and 3w-2, and there is no shop between chapter 27 and this map. Thonky says a Wyrmslayer does not work well here. Do not equip one. The Yato is the weapon the guides name for Corrin. One Fandom page says it gets through more of the half-damage skin than other weapons. Plan from the hits already measured (about 12, or 15 once promoted), not from that page's fraction. Ryoma, a bow at range 3, and Azura's song are the rest. Crits and instant-kill skills are the wrong bet: guides say the skin shrugs those off.

3. **Walk north in the quiet turns. The General is a door, not a camp.** Guides say the boss waits about five turns, then blights a quarter of the map, starting with the south-west, and each blight also heals him. Standing on it hurts. One page says 10 a turn, and that you have the turn you are on to step off. The robot already measured the same 10, and that it does not drop anyone below 1. So blight will not finish a unit. Any real hit will, once they are that low. Lure the General out, or kill him in one round, and watch the double attack from his spear. Then crowd the boss with the tough units and dual strikes. Do not stand in the blighted quarter, and do not stand on the spawn tiles.

4. **Hit him when he is on a near tile, over two turns, not in one perfect round.** The four squares he shifts between are the robot's read, not a guide's. Guides only say he is not aggressive and that you mass up in his range and rush. Trust the read for movement: he does not walk over to attack. The tools that paint his reach at 8 tiles are wrong unless they are told his real square. Strike when that square is one of the two by the opening, before the next shift. A song, a range-3 bow, and Corrin standing next to the attacker, not taking the breath, is the shape both the guides and the measured hits agree on. If the sum is short of his health, that is a second turn, not a failure. A plan that needs every hit to land is how a miss wastes the hour.

5. **Go back to turn 5. Do not continue turn 9.** Turn 9 has him deep in the alcove, a General in the door, half the army at a scratch of health, and Felicia's heal already used. The one-round plan from there needs about 82 with no miss, on tiles a berserker, a long-range golem, and a flier all reach. No guide starts the kill from that spot. Turn 5 is the last quiet moment this chain still has. From there: kill only the brave enemy who blocks the path, pair the hurt people and step them off the bad ground, put the Yato on for the hit and the stone back on if Corrin must end a turn in danger, and move the strike group north. Do not spend those turns on the east side. Do not promote unless the extra move reaches the opening that same turn. The Master Seal on turn 7 was a turn not spent walking. If the third try is still on the turn-9 idea, stop it and load the turn-5 place.

## What the robot keeps doing that players do not

- Clearing heroes, golems, and maids through turn 8, then arriving after the ground has turned.
- Hiding from a boss who does not walk, while standing where the blight and the golem's long throw can reach.
- Saving the kill for one no-miss round, instead of two ordinary ones.
- Separating a pair on the staging turn, which spends both actions.
- Treating a guide's "sacrifice the weak ones" line as a plan. That line is in the IGN page. It is not this project's rule.

Nothing here is a tested route. It is the guides, cut to the bags this army has.
