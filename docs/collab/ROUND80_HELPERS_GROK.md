# Round 80 helpers

**For the owner.** Two small helpers. The enemy turn can play faster. The forecast can show the real chance to hit. Neither changes the dice, a save, or a seed.

## Words

Heading: Helpers

Enemy phase. Choices: Normal, 2x, 4x.
Point at it: "How fast the enemy turn plays. Talks and story scenes stay at normal speed. Held Tab still wins."

True hit. Choices: Off, On.
Point at it: "Also shows the real chance to hit, such as 70 (real 79). It never changes a roll."

Under the rows: "The game's own Options line 6 (Off, Move, or All) and the Start key still skip the enemy turn. These rows do not change that."

## The three calls

Sound at 2x and 4x: keep it quiet. Fans expect turbo to be quiet. Held Tab is already quiet. Sound comes back for every talk and boss line, because those drop to normal speed. Sped-up sound is a chipmunk track, and people turn it off.

True hit: keep whole numbers, "70 (real 79)". Show "real 100" only when the hit is certain. Never show "real 0" unless it is 0. At 50 or below, show only the game's number. The two numbers are the same, and "50 (real 50)" is noise.

Boss fights: keep the default. The fight plays fast. Its lines play at normal speed. If a test shows a quote cut off, that whole forced fight plays at normal speed.

## The line for the report

True hit adds the real chance next to the number the game already shows, and it never changes whether the attack hits.
