# Revelation, chapters 25 through the ending

**For the owner.** These four maps are not the dark ones, where you cannot see the enemy. The trick here is rooms that slide, and water that keeps making new enemies until a vein freezes it. The robot should win the stated way and stop. Nobody may be lost. If a try is still going near 100 minutes, stop and keep the place.

## Which ones will cost time

1. **Chapter 25.** The rooms slide at the end of the enemy turn. One person in a doorway stops a room. The look-ahead will undo a lot.
2. **The ending.** Three fights in one map, and new enemies never stop. Clearing them is how the hour goes.
3. **Chapter 26**, only if the robot camps the pools. Freeze them and go to the boss.
4. **Chapter 27** is the short one, if the pools are sealed. The risk is starting it before the army is ready, because there is no castle after it.

Chapters 25 through 27 stay real play. The one cheat is only for the ending, and only if the real fight is stuck.

## The maps

**25. Defeat the boss.** The army starts split, north and south. Pair up so both groups fit in the small room that is there at the start. Rooms slide at the end of the enemy turn. A person standing in either side of a double door stops that room from moving. Use that on purpose, or not at all. Do not leave someone there by accident. Three veins, north then middle then south, open the boss's room. After that, his room slides and docks at the corners. Stay out of range of those doors until your own turn, or the room's people rush you. He is a fast sword user. A knife or a lance hits him more reliably than a sword. Kill him on your turn, with a fast person, and do not leave a hurt person in his reach. This is not a hidden map. You can see the rooms. The waste is waiting for the wrong room to dock.

**26. Defeat the boss.** Wide and open. Pools of water keep making new enemies, a new set every few turns, and they do not stop. A vein freezes one pool. One guide says five turns, another says six on the harder setting. The first freeze will show which. Freeze a pool just before its next set, then move. Do not farm them. Corrin carries a healing square that follows him, about three by three, and it also makes people harder to hit. Keep the frail people inside it. One guide says the singer has the same square. The boss halves damage, starts with a one-tile weapon, and is weak to magic. He can answer a second round with a thrown axe, so rush him in one turn once the pools are frozen. Hammers and beast-killers help if the army has them. Chests are not the win.

**27. Defeat the boss.** This is the last map with a castle. If the army is not ready, leave the preparations and come back. Once this fight ends, the ending starts, and there is no save between them. The veins here turn a pool into a healing tile and seal it. Unlike chapter 26, a sealed pool stays sealed. Seal them before the listed waves (about turns 10, 13, 19, and 22) instead of fighting those waves. The first turns are a crowd in the middle: kill the magic users before you leave a tough person out. The boss does not stand on a healing tile. The special sword Corrin is given before this fight does the real damage. A weapon that is good against dragons does not work on this form. Other people only chip. Do not try the one-turn trick unless a rescue staff and the singer are both in the army and the forecast says the second hit kills. Two clean turns is the safe win.

**Ending. Defeat the core.** No save, no castle, and no going back. The win is not the hands and not the head. Kill both hands first, one group each side. The head appears in the middle when both hands are dead, and it does not act on that turn. Use that turn. The core appears when the head dies, and it does not act on that turn either. Its breath is magic and reaches farther than the hands. New enemies come from the corners and change which corner after each part dies. Do not chase them. Pair the attacker, block one hit, heal from outside his range, and step back. He does not strike twice in one round, but two rounds in a row will kill someone who is not healed. Corrin's special sword, and the royal weapons if those people are deployed, are the damage. One cheat is allowed only here, and only if the hands or the core will not die in a real try.

Nothing here is a tested route. It is the guides, cut to the rule that nobody is lost.
