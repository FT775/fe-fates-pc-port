# Conquest 21–27, with the movement boost

**For the owner.** With this much movement, the win is usually one turn, before the enemy gets a turn. Staffs, new enemies, and the boss's vein never fire if the map is already over. The new danger is walking into a counter that kills someone on the way. Nobody may be lost. Do not open a chest. Do not clear the rest of the map. If a try is still going near 100 minutes, stop and keep the place. No second cheat.

## Which ones can still cost time

1. **25.** The boss does not walk out. A door opens when a retainer dies. If that door waits until the next turn, this is two turns, not one.
2. **27.** A stair opens only after the side room is cleared. His weapon reaches three tiles. Same risk: the stair may not open until the next turn.
3. **26.** Opening the south door on turn 1 puts the whole room in range. Kill only what can hit the seizer, then seize. Do not open both doors.

24, 22, and 23 should be one turn if the forecast can see the boss. 21 is a walk, not a fight.

## The maps

**21. Everyone escapes.** The red tiles are at the north end. New monsters never stop, and a healer left alone dies. Walk every person to a red tile on turn 1 and leave, Corrin last. Fight nothing. Do not send one pair ahead. The boss can be walked past. The last people must leave on the same turn. Already done this way. Repeat it.

**22. Seize the gate.** Walls block the gate. A vein opens a wall on the other lane, so a royal has to use it. Launchers hit a cluster from far away. One royal opens the wall that blocks the gate. A paired royal kills the boss on the gate. Corrin seizes, same turn. Do not walk the army into a launcher. The princess's staffs never fire if there is no enemy phase. Already done this way.

**23. Seize.** Stairs warp, and the move that was left is still there. The boss can shoot from a tile away, so standing next to him is not safe. Take the stair that still leaves move. Kill him on the throne before he shoots. Corrin seizes, same turn. Pair the attacker. Do not stand in a launcher's line on the way. Already done this way.

**24. Seize.** She stands on a vein she can use again. Hers speeds fliers and slows everyone on foot. A staff on this map halves health for good. New fliers keep coming, but only on an enemy phase you should never reach. Reach her on turn 1, before she uses the vein. Kill her. Stand on her tile. Bring a bow in the pair in case a flier blocks that tile. If the forecast says the staff can hit the seizer on the way, kill that user first, same turn, then her. Do not chase a flier.

**25. Defeat the boss.** Not a seize. He waits about 25 turns and does not walk out. Do not wait. One public guide says killing either retainer opens the path in. If the door opens at once, kill that retainer, then crowd him, same turn, from outside his sword. His sword reaches past one tile. Pair the attacker. If the door does not open until the next turn, stop on the safe side of it. Do not spend the night in the knife room. Do not lure that room with one person.

**26. Seize.** West is axes. East is magic. The south room rushes you if a door opens early, including hammers and weapons that kill beasts and fliers. Open one door only. Kill the boss and anyone the forecast says can hit the seizer. Corrin seizes, same turn. Do not open the other door. Do not open a chest. High resistance on the person who strikes him.

**27. Defeat the boss.** Not a seize. The side-room stairs open when that room's enemies are dead, and a stair leaves whatever move is left. His weapon reaches three tiles, and he halves damage. Maids can pull someone across the map, but that is an enemy-phase trick. If a stair opens at once, warp, stay outside his three tiles, and send tough paired people in until the forecast says he dies. Corrin does not have to seize. If the stair waits until the next turn, kill the room and stop outside his range. If a pull shows in the forecast before he is dead, kill that maid first. There is a save at the preparations. There is no save after. The ending starts from here, and it is a different cheat. Do not add it on this map.

Nothing here is a tested route for 24 through 27. It is the guides, cut to one turn and nobody lost.
