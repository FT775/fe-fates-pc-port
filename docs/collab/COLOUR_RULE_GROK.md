# Colour rules

1. **Hair only.** Battle hair is one number, and portrait and map hair are a pixel shift. An outfit tint fights the baked player-blue, enemy-red, and ally-green, and personal outfits already have their own colours.

2. **Hair and skin are enough.** They already stay the person's own in any class. The blue, red, or green body is how you tell the side. Do not tint it. If that class has their personal outfit, use that model. If it does not, leave the army texture.

3. **Leave enemy copies.** A boss who shares the person's row would change with them, and the chapter would show the wrong fighter. If they share a row, do not change that hair number at all.

4. **Accept it.** Two silver-haired people looking alike is honest. A marker is new interface, and it will not sit right on a picture whose hair is already baked in.
