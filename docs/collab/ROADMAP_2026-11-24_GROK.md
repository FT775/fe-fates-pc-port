# The next stretch, revised

**For the owner.** In a few weeks you should be able to hand someone a download that has no game in it. They add their own copy, press one button, and play. Today they would have to install a compiler. That is the wrong first hour. The face swap still waits until you have been asked to play a seed.

Status 2026-11-24. This replaces the first plan. Sizes use the counted bases: a lean round is about 1 hour 40, a full check is about 4 hours 25 plus about 1 hour of slow tests, an engine round is 4 to 6 hours, a release part is about 3 hours 30.

## Where I changed my mind

The full check goes last, on the program you will actually play. Checking first would certify a program the next rounds then change. One check is enough. Two would spend the 5 hours twice.

Chests are not a 7-hour table job. The items live in chapter scripts. We have no script writer. A swap that changes a word's length moves every later mark in that file. That is how those tools break. Do not rewrite scripts. When a script hands out an item, the engine looks it up in the seed's list. That never edits Seal of Flames' files. It rides in the engine batch. Fans expect chest and village items. That is a fact about what they ask for. It is not a model to copy.

The help text needs the engine build. I was wrong. It costs nothing extra because it rides in the same batch.

The licence look is small. Zakuro, the recompiler, and Zig are MIT. The recompiler already has a Recompile button and can fetch Zig. A player does not need Rust. What we do not know is whether the randomizer page, 60 fps, recolour, and Seal work in that library mode. That is the risk, not the licence.

Bringing a Citra save over is a careful copy, with a backup first. It should be earlier. The headline is a Seal of Flames save, because those players are the ones coming from Citra. Tests use fake folders only. Never your saves.

Presets and a code to paste are a lean round. The spoiler list already opens. The seed box may be enough for the paste.

## Where I still disagree

Do not have a person open all 70 robot cells. The full check should look at what this stretch changed, the six Seal bosses with the same attacker both times, and one child join if a route reaches it. The other cells stay robot-judged.

Do not open a visible test window. The library-mode proof is headless. You do the one visible "I downloaded it and pressed Recompile" yourself, as a named step.

Do not add a faster-forward step to this engine batch. Held Tab already speeds battles. The batch is full.

Do not start the face swap in the same round as the play ask. The ask is one line. If you say keep building, the next round starts the swap.

## The one big swing

The ready-made download. A program with no game code. You add your copy and press Recompile. This stretch, if the headless proof passes. If that proof fails, it slips, and we do not spend an engine OK on it.

Not this stretch: a TV layout, a mod list, sharper menus from your own pictures, mouse on the map, a cheats screen. Mouse comes after you have played, in the next stretch. One engine round then, not two now.
