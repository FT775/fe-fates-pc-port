Yes. The plan worked out each session's own baseline by itself (no `--baseline-rev`) and counted the weapon-rank
readers as "watched, never called". So for the first time no override was settled by hand, and only the new
chapter played live. The live part dropped from 14.5 min to 7 min.

| Step | Round 12 | Round 13 |

| Whole integrate | ~1 h 35 min | ~1 h 40 min |

The 1.5 h target was missed by about 10 min. Following round 12's advice, check_all got 12 lanes. Next to H6
and the live run, that was too much for this PC. The 11 older planted-bug rows got slow enough to hit their time
limits. That was only slowness: no mismatch line appeared, and all 11 passed when rerun, each inside its normal
limit. Without the rerun, integrate would have taken about 1 h 10 min. Next time: give check_all 8 lanes while H6
runs, or start the planted-bug rows first.
