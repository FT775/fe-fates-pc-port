# Randomizer: why there was no character shuffle, and what is built

**Why it wasn't there.** The page had a row "Personal skills: shuffle", and it looked like the character shuffle. It never was. It only swaps each person's personal skill with someone else's. The real character shuffle (who joins in which spot, like Recruitment in the Sacred Stones port) was never built. In F-043 we agreed to hold it back until you asked for it. You asked on 2026-11-20. The row is now called "Personal skills (shuffle = swap the skills between characters)". Its choices did not change, and the page picked up the new label without an engine build.

**Did we build other parts?** Yes. Round 55 built Children, Random promotions, Random music and Nuzlocke. Each one is code with passing tests. None of them is on the page yet: they still need a wiring round. That is why the page still says "Children (not built)".

**Built / not built, against the Sacred Stones randomizer screen:**
- **On the page now** (robot-checked, waiting for your look):
  - Randomizer and Seed
  - Growths, with its variance, min and max
  - Adjust HP growths
  - Bases, with its variance
  - Player classes (plus class trees: own side or any)
  - Royals keep their classes (the Fates version of "Include lords")
  - Boss classes and Enemy classes (with the no-stuck-enemies rule)
  - Enemy weapons (on/off only, not a chance setting)
  - Shops
  - Castle rewards
  - Also, rows Sacred Stones does not have: Personal skills, Skill slots, Starting items.
- **Code done, not on the page yet:** Random promotions (two random Master Seal choices, plus random Heart Seal classes), Random music, Children, Nuzlocke. Nuzlocke also needs engine hooks before it works in the game.
- **Designed, not built:** the character shuffle, with these rows: Recruitment (off / route / any), Look (keep the face / full identity), Class (own / slot), Stats (autolevel / slot).
- **Not built, no design yet:** Class movement, Assign evenly, Class growths / Class bases, Enemy growths, Boss stats, Weapon might / hit / weight / uses, Weapon traits, Enemy drops, Better enemy weapons as a chance setting, a third promotion choice (that one needs an engine change).
- **Not in Fates:** Constitution, Affinity, Monster classes and Monster promos, FE7/FE6 classes, Keep colours, Tower & Ruins.
- **Not needed:** Master Seals only (the Fates Master Seal already promotes every class).
- **Fixed by the rules instead of a row:** Match gender is always on, and Azura always keeps her class.
- **Rewards is partial:** chests and villages are in the chapter scripts and are not changed.

**The shuffle plan:** 9 counted rounds, with this design round as round 1, so 8 more. It could be 11 if two expected problems happen: a face missing an expression, and the Conquest 9 recorded presses not fitting.
- Each join spot stays in the story; only the person in it changes. No story script is ever edited. This avoids the crashes the public Fates randomizers have.
- At first, men only take men's spots and women only take women's.
- Corrin, Azura, Gunter and the royals never move. Children stay out.
- Nyx and Shura stay in their own spots until Conquest 9 and Conquest 16 pass the robot.
- The first look you can play ("keep the face") comes after plan round 3. Full identity (the new person's name, portrait, model and voice) comes after round 6.
