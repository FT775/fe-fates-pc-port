## What to run first

1. The split, both ways, from that shared save if it allows both choices. If it allows only one, the other side is a new game.
2. Conquest chapter 6 is the cheaper first clear: defeat 4 enemies, no turn clock. Do not fight Ryoma. Revelation chapter 6 is the same map shape with a 5-turn clock. Run them side by side. If one save is stuck, finish Conquest 6 first.
3. On the first preparations of either new route, open Equip Skills on one unit and leave. That is a reader, not a chapter. See the last section.
4. The first vein Birthright's list does not have is Conquest chapter 8, one lake. That needs chapter 6 and chapter 7 first. Chapter 7 is a rout with a one-tile choke, not the wall.
5. The first talk the robot must not skip is Revelation chapter 11: Corrin talks to Saizo.
6. The script reader Birthright can never meet is Revelation chapter 12. Do not start that chapter until 6 through 11 are recorded.
7. Do not capture. Do not grind a marriage. Do not open X015 unless it is already on the world map. If it is not, write "not here" and stop. No English title was on the pages read. Do not guess one. The DLC chapter "I: In Endless Dreams" is a xenologue, not `CID_X015`. [docs/ROUTES.md; the chapter list's xenologue table]

## Castle, between chapters

My Castle is the hub before a chapter once it is unlocked. An invasion is listed at chapter 9 on all three routes, so the castle exists by then. The Conquest chapter 7 page says that after that battle you can spend a second Dragon Vein point on the Dusk Armory or the Staff Store, so the build menu is open by the end of chapter 7. The page does not say the castle opens at the end of chapter 6. Do not hunt it before chapter 7. [My Castle, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/My_Castle; A Dragon's Decree, both read 2026-11-15]

- Conquest facilities do not award experience. Do not visit the arena or the mess hall expecting a level. Birthright and Revelation do have experience activities. Still do not grind.
- Conquest can build the Nohrian set only (Dusk Armory, Staff Store, Crystal Mine, and the rest of that column). Revelation can build both sets. Birthright's Dawn Armory is not on a Conquest castle.
- Every castle is given one food spot and one ore spot of that route's set, at random. Revelation is given four: one Hoshidan food, one Nohrian food, one Hoshidan ore, one Nohrian ore. The page does not say which. Do not assume Pearl or Crystal.
- Food is there in the morning. Ore is there in the evening. The day moves after a battle, or after six real hours. One point per completed battle.
- If a spot is already on the castle, one harvest is enough, same rule as Birthright. Do not swap. A swap takes five. Do not pet. A talk with an exclamation mark can raise a support or hand over a resource. The robot's castle walk has not reached that talk. Do not add a piece whose only job is the petting scene.

## Conquest

Chapter 6 is 20 by 22. No items. Xander, Camilla, Leo, and Elise fight beside you and cannot gain experience, cannot trade, and leave at the end. Use Xander to stand in front of Ryoma. Do not have him start the fight. Win on the other four: Hinoka, Takumi, Yukimura, Sakura. Do not kill Ryoma. [Embrace the Dark, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Embrace_the_Dark]

Chapter 7 is 19 by 20. No items. Preparations deploys two units, and Corrin is one of them, so the other is the servant unless a bonus unit is already in the army. Do not go get one. Faceless in the south start moving on turns 2 and 3. There is a one-tile choke in the bottom-right. Clear the south, then have the bulky unit hold that tile. Do not leave a Faceless alive if one hit would finish it: two of them carry a skill that only hurts a unit left standing. After the clear, the first paralogue (Tragic Start) opens, plus one more if Corrin is male and two more if Corrin is female. Those are not child maps. Do not detour. [A Dragon's Decree, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/A_Dragon%27s_Decree]

Chapter 8's size was not in the extract. The plot says Odin and Niles arrive during the fight. The join line (talk or automatic) was cut off. If a talk command is on a green unit, take it. Then defeat Kilma and stop. Use the lake vein once. That is the new effect. Do not stay to clear the village after Kilma falls. [Cold Reception, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Cold_Reception; the vein page]

Chapter 9 is 30 columns by 15 rows (the row count was cut at "15 row" on the extract; do not invent the rest). The plot says Corrin recruits Nyx during the fight. If a talk command is offered, take it. Azura is a join, not a boss. Haitaka can be captured. Do not capture. Seize the throne. The acid-rain vein drops HP to 1, including your own if you stand in it. Do not use it on your own tiles. [Another Trial, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Another_Trial]

Chapter 10 is the first real wall. The start is 1 to 10 of yours plus 3 who join, against 19 enemies plus 29 more. The size was not in the extract. Takumi is the boss and he does not have to die. Hold the flashing green tiles for 11 turns. Do not leave one empty. Do not chase him. If a push would lose a unit, wait. Camilla, Selena, and Beruka join; the page's plot says Camilla arrives with her retainers. If a talk command is offered, take it. The river vein lets grounded units cross. Use it only if a tile of yours is on the wrong side. Do not cross just to fight. [Unhappy Reunion, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Unhappy_Reunion]

Chapter 11's size and turn limit were not on the page read. The strategy note says Kaze joins at the end, and he is the only ninja on this route. Defeat Hinoka. Do not climb past her for extra fights. No vein on the public vein list. [Rainbow Sage (Conquest), https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Rainbow_Sage_(Conquest_chapter)]

Chapter 12 is 19 by 28, and the clock is 16 turns. Elise cannot be deployed. Laslow and Peri are already on the map at turn 1. Win by Corrin reaching the escape, or by defeating Ryoma. Escape is the cheaper plan. Do not fight Ryoma unless the escape is blocked. The vein breaks every pot. Pots are medicine or poison, and the effect hits every unit within two spaces, yours included. Do not break a pot your own units are standing on. One attack on a pot is allowed, for the reader note below, and then stop breaking them. Two chests sit on the map. Do not add turns for them if the clock is close. [Bitter Intrigue, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Bitter_Intrigue]

Conquest has no free experience from the castle, and the guest royals on chapter 6 cannot gain any. The early maps are not a grind. The wall is chapter 10, not chapter 7. The cheap tactic there is to wait on the green tiles.

Chapter 6 is 20 by 22. Same river vein as Birthright chapter 6 and Conquest chapter 6. Not a new vein. Azura can sing. The Nohrian boss is across the river and does not rush you, so do not wait for him. Dry the river on turn 2, after the near side is dealt with, so the far side can reach you before turn 5. Do not use the vein on turn 1 if your own units still need the water as a wall. [Into the Ground, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Into_the_Ground]

Chapter 7 has no vein. Rooms appear only when a unit reaches the end of the one before, and a dead-end tile warps that unit back to the start of the room. Do not step on a dead end. Gunter joins at the north end. The boss has no range, so the servant can chip it with Corrin standing next to them. Seize. Do not loop the map for chests. The size was not in the extract. [Unspeakable World, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Unspeakable_World]

Chapter 8 reuses the Birthright chapter 7 map. The join method for Kaze, Sakura, Hana, and Subaki was not in the extract. If a talk command is offered, take it, then defeat Yukimura. The healing-tile vein is the same kind of effect as Birthright chapter 7. Not a new vein. Size not in the extract. [Traitor's Brand, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Traitor%27s_Brand; the vein page]

Chapter 9 is 25 by 29. Winds push your units five spaces at the end of a turn, up or down by colour. Do not end a turn in a wind if the landing is off the map or on an enemy. Rinkah is already yours on turn 1. Hayato joins when the map is over, so do not hunt him. Seize. Do not fight Fuga if the throne is open. Reinforcements start on turn 5. The vein also pushes enemies. Use it once, off your own tiles. [Wanderer, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Wanderer]

Chapter 10 is 23 by 18. Ice blocks hide enemies. Breaking one block breaks the 3 by 3 around it. A revealed enemy can act on the next enemy phase, so do not open a block you cannot fight. Takumi, Oboro, and Hinata join after the map. Seize Zola's tile. Do not chase the units under the ice after the seize is open. [Voice of a God, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Voice_of_a_God]

Chapter 11 is 25 by 13. This is the talk. Corrin talks to Saizo. That talk also brings Orochi and Reina. Do not rout the map before that talk. Kagero joins at the end, so do not hunt her. The vein removes spike traps. Use it once if a trap is on your path. The map is the Birthright chapter 10 map. The spike effect is the reverse of that chapter's vein. [Mutual Enemies, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Mutual_Enemies]

Chapter 12 is the script reader, below. Win is defeat Flora. Camilla, Selena, and Beruka join; the join line was not in the extract. If a talk command is offered, take it. The vein makes ice bridges between the boats. Use it once. That use is the map's own vein command, which Birthright already met. It is not the script reader. Size and turn limit were not in the extract. Do not continue to chapter 13. [Frozen Sea, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Frozen_Sea; the vein page]
