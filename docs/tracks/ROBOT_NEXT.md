## Why the smithy was not in the list

The castle tutorial, on the chapter 7 guide's page, makes you build something. The only choices it names are a Dawn Armory or a Rod Shop. You have one Dragon Vein point, and a building spends it. Points come from completing a battle or visiting another castle. [Gamer Guides, Birthright chapter 7, Nathan Garvin, https://www.gamerguides.com/fire-emblem-fates-birthright/guide/main-campaign/chapters-6-12/chapter-7-a-vow-upheld, read 2026-11-12]

The smithy is not in that list. The public object table says a level-1 smithy needs chapter 8 completed, then costs 1 point. It forges the route's own weapons up to +2. Level 2 needs chapter 14 completed (up to +4). Level 3 needs chapter 20 completed (up to +7). Each of those is also 1 point. Birthright can build it. [Fire Emblem Wiki, My Castle/Objects, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/My_Castle/Objects, table rows read 2026-11-12]

So a castle visit through chapter 7 is not a failed forge. The building is not available yet.

## Support pacing

The robot already opened one C support. `ROBOT_PLAN.md` does not ask for a second. Do not grind chapter 8 for supports if the forge is the missing exit. Attack Stance and Guard Stance need two units on the same fight, not a higher rank.

If that C is not on the save, use one fast pair and finish it in the chapter 8 map. Do not start a second pair on that map.

Points, from Serenes Forest's Hoshido pages, read 2026-11-12:

- A fast pair needs 2 points for C. A medium pair needs 3. [https://serenesforest.net/fire-emblem-fates/hoshidan-characters/supports/support-growth/]
- Each pair can gain at most 3 points on one map. Points round to a whole number at the end of the map. A fight with the partner present (paired or adjacent) is +3/6 of a point. Four of those round to 2, which is a fast C. Six of them hit the cap of 3, which is a medium C. [https://serenesforest.net/fire-emblem-fates/hoshidan-characters/supports/support-basics/]
- If one unit fights beside several partners, only the top pair keeps its points. The second pair is cut to 2, the third to 1, and the rest to 0. A tie goes to whoever is higher in the support list.
- On Casual, if either unit falls, that pair loses the points from that map.

Fast pairs that do not wait on a later Hoshido recruit: the avatar with Jakob, with Felicia, or with Azura. Felicia with Jakob is also fast. Silas has no fast line. [same support-growth page] Use one of those only if both units are already in the army. Kaze's only fast partner on that page is Saizo. Do not wait for him.
