# Birthright chapters 10 to 14

ELI5 / ETA: The robot has cleared chapter 9. This is the next five Birthright maps, and which side-maps open beside them. Nothing is built.

Status 2026-11-15. Public pages. No game file opened. Names only. Same card as `ROBOT_PLAN.md`: Normal, Casual, three failed rebuilds or four hours on one piece means skip it and keep the last good save. Do not continue to chapter 15.

Cl is Classic. Ca is Casual. Cp is a capture. The robot plays Casual. Treat a dead army as a failed piece anyway.

## What to run first

1. Chapter 10. Azura talks to Takumi before anyone attacks him. Then rout. Do not use the spike vein if your own units are standing on the traps.
2. Chapter 11. Corrin talks to Reina. Then rout. One use of the wind vein if a flier is the problem.
3. Chapter 12. Corrin escapes. Do not fight Garon. Do not walk up to Xander.
4. Chapter 13. Rout. Do not use the thunder vein on your own side.
5. Chapter 14. Defeat Daniela and stop. Do not capture her.
6. No new paralogue opens because these five were cleared. Do not marry to open one. Do not hunt a skill scroll.

## The five maps

Chapter 10. Takumi starts as an enemy, near your side, on turn 1. Azura talks to him, or he joins when the map is already over. Do the talk. Do not let a unit attack him first. Kagero joins at the end, so do not hunt her. The three veins turn on traps. The page says the traps hurt units each turn, and it does not say the traps spare your side. Do not stand on one, and do not turn one on if a unit of yours is on it. Kotaro is the boss. One chest is on the map. Do not add a piece whose only job is the chest. [Ninja Village, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Ninja_Village]

Chapter 11. The fight is on a ship, 1 to 12 of yours plus 1, against 17 plus 6. Reina arrives as a green unit. Corrin talks to her. Do not rout the map before that talk. The vein slows fliers. Use it once if a flier is the threat, then leave it. The item table was not in the extract. Do not stay for drops. [To the Sea, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/To_the_Sea]

Chapter 12 is the one map in this stretch that is not a rout and not a boss kill. 2 to 12 of yours, against 27 plus 3. Kaden is already yours on turn 1, and he is forced on. Garon does not move. He has Dragonskin. Stay out of his range. On Normal, Xander and the two with him do not move unless you walk up to them. Do not. The vein is two squares east of the chest. Use it only if Corrin needs the water to reach the escape. A flier can carry Corrin to the escape faster. That is the cheap plan. The chest and the dropped hammer are not the exit. [Dark Reunion, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Dark_Reunion]

Chapter 13. Normal is 24 enemies. Hard is 36 and Lunatic is 38. The robot stays on Normal. The plot says Ryoma and Scarlet appear after Camilla falls. The join line was cut off in the extract. If a talk command is offered before that, take it. The thunder vein drops HP to 1, and the vein page does not say it spares your side. Do not use it on a tile of yours. Rout, and do not chase Camilla into a bad tile. [Another Hope, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Another_Hope]

Chapter 14. Normal starts at 16 plus 17 more. The map is a wall. Win is Daniela, not a rout. When she falls, stop. She can be captured. Do not capture. The rock-slide vein hits enemies in range for 10. Use it once if your own units are not in that range. The item table was not in the extract. [Light Scatters, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Light_Scatters]
