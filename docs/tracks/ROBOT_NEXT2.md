## One ore

Birthright's castle is generated with one Hoshidan ore spot already placed. Other ore types are not available in that castle. [Fire Emblem Wiki, My Castle/Objects, gathering spots, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/My_Castle/Objects, read 2026-11-13]

A level-1 ore spot yields one piece and needs no Dragon Vein point and no chapter. It refills in the evening. The day moves on after a battle, or after enough real time. [same table, Pearl Spring and the other Hoshidan spots yield ×1; Serenes Forest, Resources, https://serenesforest.net/fire-emblem-fates/my-castle/resources/, read 2026-11-13]

The page does not say which Hoshidan ore was placed. Pearl is the one for a katana. Coral, Lapis, Quartz, Jade, and Amber are the others. [same Serenes page]

The smithy turns five of one ore into one of another. One piece cannot be swapped into Pearl. [Fire Emblem Wiki, My Castle, smithy, https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/My_Castle, read 2026-11-13]

## Checkpoints

1. Return to the castle. Harvest the ore spot that is already there. Do not build a second one. Do not spend a point. Do not start chapter 9.
2. If the spot is empty, fight one battle (a scout is enough; do not clear chapter 9) and harvest again. Evening is what refills it.
3. Read the ore key. A missing key is not zero. Zero is a present key whose count is 0. [review, `docs/collab/reviews/REVIEW_2026-11-13.md`]
4. If that one piece matches a pair the army already holds (two of the same weapon, same holder, same rank), open the smithy and forge once. That is the exit. If it is Pearl and the pair is two katanas, that is the one the save was blocked on.
5. If the piece matches no pair, write "ore, no pair" and stop. Do not swap. Do not visit another castle. Do not clear chapter 9 to look.
