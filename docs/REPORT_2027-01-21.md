## The drift answer (Revelation 9)
- **Cause found (corrects F-058):** the swapped battle identity (the AID_ pointers: model, outfit and battle voice set). The first time a swapped unit is hit (frame 8,244), the game's random number roller makes one roll fewer. The exact link through the sound set is likely but was not traced to the line of code.
- **What happens on screen:** the runs stay together until about frame 16,500. There a battle plays out differently, and later recorded presses land in the wrong menus.
- **Proven NOT the cause:** names, voices, the dialogue trap, faces, gender bytes and file sizes. A test run of "full swap minus AID" stayed in step for the whole 183,000-frame recording.
- **Not fixed:** it is the game's own behaviour, so a player sees no fault. Removing the AID swap would remove the battle look. That would be a design choice for you, not a fix.
- **Round 76:** judges long recordings with the test-only "full minus AID" variant, checks battle looks on short pieces from chapter-start saves, and traces the random roller against the swap-off twin. Anything after the first difference that depends on a battle counts as not reached.
